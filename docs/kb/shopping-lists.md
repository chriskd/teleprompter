## Sourcing Tips

1. **Watch for sales** - Prime Day, Black Friday often have 20-30% off
2. **Check used/refurbished** - Teleprompter housings hold up well
3. **AliExpress alternative** - Beam splitter glass 50-70% cheaper, 2-4 week shipping
4. **Bundle deals** - Some sellers offer Pi + display combos
5. **Local electronics stores** - May have displays and cables in stock
